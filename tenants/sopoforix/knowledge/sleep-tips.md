# Sleep Guidance

General wellness information only. This is not medical advice, and the agent
must always show the medical disclaimer with content from this document.

## Building a Wind-Down Routine

A consistent wind-down routine helps most people fall asleep faster. Aim for
the same bedtime and wake time every day, including weekends. In the last
hour before bed, choose calm activities such as reading, gentle stretching,
or a warm shower.

## Bedroom Environment

Keep the bedroom cool (around 65-68 degrees Fahrenheit), dark, and quiet.
Blackout curtains and a fan or white-noise machine help many sleepers.
Reserve the bed for sleep so your brain associates it with rest.

## Caffeine, Screens, and Naps

Avoid caffeine within 8 hours of bedtime and heavy meals within 2 to 3 hours.
Bright screens late at night can delay sleepiness; dim them or stop using
them an hour before bed. If you nap, keep it under 30 minutes and before
mid-afternoon.

## When to Seek Help

If poor sleep lasts more than a few weeks, or if you experience loud snoring
with pauses in breathing, extreme daytime sleepiness, or symptoms that worry
you, please talk with a qualified clinician. These can signal conditions that
deserve real medical care.
