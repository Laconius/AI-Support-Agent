# Store Policies

Approved policy language. The agent must not promise anything
beyond what is written here.

## Shipping

Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business
days in the continental US. A tracking number is emailed when the order
ships, and customers can also request it from support with their order number.

## 100-Night Sleep Trial

Every mattress topper and pillow includes a 100-night sleep trial starting on
the delivery date. Customers may request a return or exchange at any point
during the trial. We ask customers to try the product for at least 14 nights
first, because adjustment takes time.

## Returns and Refunds

Returns within the 100-night trial receive a full refund to the original
payment method once the item is picked up or dropped off. Refunds are
processed within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
Sheet sets can be returned unwashed within 30 days. Final decisions on
disputed refunds are made by a human support teammate, not by the AI agent.
