# Product Guide

Approved product facts for customer support. Keep this file accurate:
the agent quotes it directly.

## CloudRest Pillow

The CloudRest Pillow comes in Standard and Queen sizes. The outer cover is
machine washable on a cold, gentle cycle; the inner core should not be washed.
Air-dry the cover only. The fill is shredded gel memory foam, and the loft can
be adjusted by removing fill through the inner zipper.

## NightEase Mattress Topper

The NightEase Mattress Topper is available in Twin, Full, Queen, and King.
It offers medium firmness with a 3-inch gel foam layer. It fits mattresses up
to 14 inches deep using the elastic corner straps. Spot-clean only; do not
machine wash the topper.

## Breathable Sheet Set

The Breathable Sheet Set is woven from long-staple cotton with a percale
weave. Sizes: Twin, Full, Queen, King. Machine wash cold and tumble dry low.
The set includes one fitted sheet, one flat sheet, and two pillowcases
(one for Twin).

## Warranty

All products carry a 3-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects
such as seam failure or foam that does not recover its shape. The warranty
does not cover normal wear, stains, or damage from improper washing.
